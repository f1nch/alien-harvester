# Tendon Stalker

Chosen player alien lineage: hatchling, juvenile, adult, apex. Burrower and Weaver concepts are retained for possible future cosmetic variants or alternate playthroughs.

## Hatchling anatomy

- Low crouched body, wedge skull, split jaw and swept-back chitin.
- Two long capture appendages, four short walking legs, one tapered tail.
- Charcoal-plum dry fibrous tissue, muted chitin, ivory hooks.
- Mint forehead seam and recessed chest core remain recognizable through evolution.
- Evolution adds organs, limbs, armor and posture changes rather than only size.

## Reference limitations

`04-tendon-stalker-hatchling-views.png` was created with built-in ImageGen using the selected evolution sheet as a design reference. It is concept guidance, not an exact orthographic blueprint. Overlap and limb placement vary between views, particularly in the side view. Blender should enforce the fixed anatomy above and use one consistent set of proportions. Reduce fine spikes and layered surface details for the browser game's camera and performance needs.

The earlier rounded and angular Blender concepts predate this chosen lineage and are preserved as explorations.
